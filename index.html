What I work on

  • Graph analytics
  • Performance engineering
  • Green computing
  • Memory systems
  • HPC

Graph workloads are irregular and memory-bound: they spend much of their time, and their power, waiting on memory rather than computing. I build algorithms that touch less memory, and runtimes that stop wasting energy while they wait.

I’m part of the Kelvin Living Lab, which is working towards net-zero high-performance computing on Queen’s Kelvin-2 supercomputer.

  • Up to 11.49×faster subgraph search with OrbitSI
  • Up to 21%less energy with URJA
  • Up to 22.65×faster orbit counting with NI-ORCA
